What We Do

Our Investigative Services

Seven specialist disciplines. One uncompromising standard.

01
⚖️
Heir Tracing & Unclaimed Estates
"Every estate has a rightful heir. We find them."
When a person dies intestate, their estate passes to the Crown unless rightful heirs can be identified. We specialise in tracing living relatives through genealogical research across UK and international registries — Companies House, FreeBMD, the Bona Vacantia list, Duchy of Lancaster rolls, and international probate records. We reconstruct family trees across multiple generations, locating beneficiaries who may not know an inheritance awaits them.
No Win · No Fee · 60% Contingency on Recovered Estate
02
💍
Matrimonial Surveillance
"When silence speaks louder than words — we listen."
Suspecting infidelity is one of the most painful experiences a person can face. Before making life-altering decisions, you deserve facts — not speculation. Our investigators conduct discreet covert surveillance documenting movements, meetings and behaviour with photographic and video evidence legally admissible in UK family courts. We track unexplained absences, undisclosed contacts, and behavioural anomalies — providing a clear evidential record within 5–10 working days. Fully RIPA and GDPR compliant.
Fixed Daily Rate · Full Evidence Pack & Report Included
03
🧭
Missing Persons Tracing
"Found. Whatever it takes."
People go missing for many reasons — estrangement, mental health crises, debt, or deliberate disappearance. Whether you are searching for a lost family member, birth parent, estranged sibling, or a debtor who has vanished, our investigators access address history databases, electoral roll records, financial indicators, and field contacts to establish current whereabouts. We locate individuals across the UK, Europe, the Gulf, and internationally. Sensitive cases handled with full compassion and discretion.
Fixed Investigation Fee · Secure Location Report Delivered
04
🏢
Corporate Due Diligence
"Know who you're dealing with — before you sign anything."
Before entering a partnership, investment, or acquisition, our corporate intelligence unit conducts in-depth background checks on companies and their principals: directorship history, CCJ records, dissolved company analysis, beneficial ownership structures, and international litigation history. We cross-reference Companies House with Dubai DED, UAE court records, US SEC filings, and international business registries to reveal hidden directorships and red flags that standard checks miss entirely.
Tiered Pricing by Scope · Report in 5 Working Days
05
📋
Process Serving & Legal Support
"Documents served. Affidavit provided. Case proceeds."
Serving legal documents on evasive individuals requires both investigative skill and legal knowledge. Our certified process servers operate across England, Wales, Scotland, the UAE, and internationally under Hague Convention protocols. We serve claim forms, injunctions, divorce petitions, court orders and statutory demands — providing sworn affidavits of service for court proceedings. Turnaround from instruction to confirmed service: 24–72 hours in most cases.
Fixed Fee Per Service · Sworn Affidavit Included
06
🛡️
Background Verification
"Trust is earned. We help you verify it."
Comprehensive background checks: identity verification, employment history confirmation, academic credential validation, criminal record checks (DBS standard and enhanced), financial conduct review, and social media intelligence. Used by employers, landlords, legal professionals and private individuals across the UK and Gulf. Pre-nuptial investigations available for clients entering significant relationships — verifying financial representations and identifying prior proceedings. Delivered within 48–72 hours.
Standard · Enhanced · Premium Tiers Available

Distressed Asset Recovery & Liquidation
"Seized. Valued. Sold. Proceeds distributed."
We identify, assess, and liquidate high-value distressed assets — supercars, luxury real estate, and business assets — seized by banks and courts in Dubai and the USA. Working as authorised intermediaries between financial institutions and buyers, we facilitate rapid liquidation at below-market prices, generating returns for all parties. Access to our Asset Marketplace below. VIP investors gain first-look access to high-value opportunities before public release.
